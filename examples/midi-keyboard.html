Live MIDI keyboard

Connect a MIDI keyboard, click Enable MIDI, and play. Held notes light up together. This is the display inserted into Reveal slides by \midikeyboard.

C3–C5 corresponds to MIDI notes 48–72; middle C is C4 (60). The display follows your keys, including when the sustain pedal is down. Reset lights clears a missed note release. No sound is generated.

Use a browser with Web MIDI support and open this page over HTTPS or at http://localhost.
